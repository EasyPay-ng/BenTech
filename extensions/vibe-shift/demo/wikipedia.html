Makurdi is the capital city of Benue State in the Middle Belt of Nigeria, sitting on the south bank of the Benue River. It is a river port and a market town, and it has been both for longer than it has been a capital.

History

The town grew around the ferry crossing. Trade in yams, soya beans and oranges moved north and salt moved south, and the bridge that replaced the ferry made the crossing cheaper without making it less of a decision.

Economy

Markets, transport and a growing fintech sector. Most employment is informal and most of it is not counted by anyone.
